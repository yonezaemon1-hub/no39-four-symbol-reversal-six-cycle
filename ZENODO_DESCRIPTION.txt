Ryutaro Yonezu, Yonezu Research Lab.

For a stationary continuous-time homogeneous six-cycle with clockwise rate a>0, counterclockwise rate b>0, deterministic binary labels 001011, and equal sampling intervals, this note derives an exact expression for p(0011)-p(1100). A strictly positive polynomial factor proves that this one pair of four-consecutive-symbol population probabilities witnesses every nonresonant time-reversal asymmetry in the specified model. At the resonant intervals, the entire sampled process is time-reversal symmetric. Length four is sharp because lengths one through three are reversal symmetric for every stationary binary process.

The short-word obstruction, periodic-sampling ambiguity and eventual scenery-reconstruction implications are established prior context.
